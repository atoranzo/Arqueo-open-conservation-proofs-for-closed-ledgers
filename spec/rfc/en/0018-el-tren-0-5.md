## Motivation

Two limits that `SECURITY.md` declares (3.6 and 3.10, §670) and that only close by changing the
format of the proof. They are joined in a single train to pay a single break, as the v2.0 blueprint
asked.

1. **The frozen tree is not bound in the AIR.** In the five circuits with a frozen phase
   (`circuit_send`, `circuit_send_v2`, `circuit_claim`, `circuit_claim_v2`, `circuit_burn`), what
   the AIR proves is that SOME position of the frozen tree has an empty leaf, not the holder's. §511
   added two accumulators of the position and their equality, but the one of the frozen climb does
   not accumulate the same bit the multiplexer of that climb reads, and the equality does not force
   the holder's position to be walked. Measured in the re-triage on `ed0f96b`: the trace of a frozen
   account that walks another position gives `prove: Ok` and `verify: Ok`. The layer stops it with
   `AccountFrozen` (S487): the money is safe; what is missing is that the proof alone says so.
2. **The custodian and governance keys are of one element.** `derive_custodian_id` and
   `derive_governor_id` derive the identity from a single Goldilocks element: about 64 bits, and an
   offline attack of about 2^63 evaluations against a public identity (ESTIMATED). The spending key
   already has a wide version since entry 15; these do not.

Since §707 the train also carries a proposal that does not come out of those two limits: that **a
proof says what it is** —its format version, its family, the version of its AIR and its options
profile—, so that what the cut changes is rejected by name and not with a winterfell error. It is
D-G, with its stage, E3, and it has its section, below.

## Design

**E1 — The two accumulators read the bit their multiplexer uses.** (§679: also the accounts one; see
D-A.) In the five circuits,
`COL_FACC` accumulates in each cycle the SAME bit of `COL_FBIT` that the multiplexer of the frozen
climb reads to choose the order of the siblings (today one reads `next` and the other `current`, in
different rows of the cycle), or else `COL_FBIT` is fixed constant along the cycle with a transition
constraint, so that there are not two bits to choose from. The equality of `COL_IACC` and `COL_FACC`
in the row §511 measured stays the same. Falsifiers: the adversarial trace of the re-triage (frozen,
neighbouring position) has to stop verifying in all five, and the mutations of each new slot have to
fall.

**E1, built (§680).** In the five circuits the step of each accumulator goes in the rows of the
multiplexer of its climb —`link_place + link_merkle` those of accounts, `frozen_entry + frozen_link`
those of the frozen tree— and adds `next[COL_BIT]` and `next[COL_FBIT]`, the bit the multiplexer
reads. With that the two periodic step columns of §511 were superfluous: they are removed, 48 -> 46
in `circuit_send`. Ten falsifiers, two per circuit (attacks F and I), fall with the rule of before
and with the one that reads the bit of the selector's own row.
**E2 — Four-element keys for custodians and governance.** `derive_custodian_id_wide` and
`derive_governor_id_wide`, the mould of `derive_public_id_wide`: the key is a `Digest` and the
identity is `native_merge(dominio, clave)`. The threshold and governance circuits carry the key in
four columns instead of one. The roots of the custodian and governance sets change, because the
leaves change: a new set is constituted with wide keys.

**E2, first piece (§683): the derivations.** `derive_custodian_id_wide`,
`derive_governor_id_wide`, `build_custodian_set_wide` and `build_governance_set_wide`, with a single
tree constructor for the four (`conjunto_de_hojas`). No AIR changes yet. The map of E2 measured
where the key goes in the eight circuits that carry it: the domain in `state[4]` and the key in
`state[8]`, with `state[9..12]` unasserted, so four elements fit without one more hash cycle. The
exception is the nullifier of `circuit_threshold_single_nullifier`, the only one of them that goes
over the wire: today it fills the whole rate (`[NDOM, k, 0, 0 | op]`) and with the wide key it does
not fit; it is composed in two hashes, the mould of `native_nullifier_wide`.

**E2, second piece (§684): the threshold of the wire.** `circuit_threshold_single_nullifier`
(`NullifierThresholdAir`, the only authority circuit that goes over the wire) carries the key in
four columns and binds the four elements to the identity; the nullifier is `H(H(dominio, clave),
operación)`, in two hashes (rows 40-47 and 48-55). A trace of 21 columns and 57 constraints. The
narrow keys are still valid, padded with zeros: the layer does not change.

**E2, third piece (§701): the layer with wide sets.** The layer only receives the two roots, so
it does not change: what was missing was exercising it with keys of four non-zero elements. Three
tests of the layer: a ledger constituted with wide keys issues, issues to a pending one, recovers,
freezes and rotates its custodians to another wide set, from which the incoming ones issue; on that
ledger, a key of a single element —the only thing the 2^63 search reaches—, here even with the true
first element of two keys and the path of its leaf, neither issues nor rotates (`NotTheIssuer`, and
with the root set by hand the proof does not verify), and the whole key does; and a ledger of narrow
keys rotates its custodians to wide keys with the signature of its narrow governance. With E2
built on the wire it is declared, in `SECURITY.md` 3.10, what is still worth one element:
a ledger of narrow keys; the node in `dev`, which opens with the roots of the suite, of one-element
keys, and does not change before the cut, because the `0.4` vectors carry its custodian root; and
the other seven circuits that carry an authority key, which do not go over the wire and are not
widened here.

**E3 — The proof says what it is: the v2 mark (D-G, in proposal state, §707).** The mark of the
trace meta goes from `arqueo:oculta:1` and m, 19 bytes, to version 2, of 24 fixed bytes, with the
family, the version of the AIR and the profile; the judge reads it before anything else and names
the cause of each rejection, the same in the kit and in `tools/segunda`. It enters in the seal of
the cut and not before. The format, what enters the Fiat-Shamir seed, the order of the judge, the
second implementation and the planned vectors are in the «D-G» section, below. Nothing of it is
built.

## Compatibility

It breaks the wire: the proofs of the seven touched circuits do not verify with the AIRs of
`zkssl/0.4`, and the head signs roots of different sets. (⚠️ Corrected in §701: the AIRs of the wire
that E1 and E2 touch are six, the five of E1 and `NullifierThresholdAir`, which carries the key of
the two sets; `GovernanceAir` does not go over the wire. And the head signs different roots only if
the ledger is constituted with wide keys: with the narrow ones, padded, the roots are the same,
§683.) `zkssl/0.5`, with the `0.4` vectors preserved under their version, as rule 2 of the PROCESO
asks. The kit verifies no send, claim, burn or threshold proofs, so its reading of envelopes does
not change; what does change is the wire version it accepts. (§707: with the E3 that D-G proposes
the bytes of all proofs change, also those of the kit's five families, because all carry the v2
mark; what kit 0.5 rejects of the earlier versions, and with what cause, D-G says.)

- **D-A — the multiplexer's bit, accumulated (variant A).** Measured before choosing, with the
  session's bench on `circuit_send` (the two variants and the base, in release, the same honest
  trace):

  | | constraints | periodic | `prove` (median of 7) | `verify` (median of 31) | proof |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | base (`zkssl/0.4`) | 208 | 48 | 1.011 ms | 4,36 ms | 79.144 B |
  | A: the accumulator's step in the multiplexer's row, reading `next` | 208 | 48 | 924 ms | 4,53 ms | 80.199 B |
  | B: the bit constant in the cycle | 210 | 50 | 1.005 ms | 4,38 ms | 76.451 B |

  The size of the proof and the times move within the noise of the queries: the two cost the
  same. Both close the two measured attacks (below), with `UnsatisfiedTransitionConstraintError`.
  A wins because it adds no constraint and no periodic column: it changes the rows of the two
  periodic step columns and the bit their two constraints read.

  **What the bench measured besides.** The accumulator of the ACCOUNTS climb (`COL_IACC`) has the
  same offset as the frozen one: it adds `COL_BIT` in row +7 of each cycle, and the multiplexer of
  that climb reads it in row +0 of the next. Attack F (the re-triage one): `COL_FBIT` of row +7 with
  the bits of the frozen holder P and the frozen climb through the free neighbour Q: the base
  accepts it. Attack I (new): the accounts climb through P and `COL_BIT` of row +7 with the bits of
  Q, so that `IACC` says Q: the base also accepts it. The two variants reject them. **E1 binds the
  two accumulators**, not only the frozen one.
- **D-B and D-C — the two wide keys; governance, with a new ledger (the author's decision, §683).**
  In §679 the session wrote that what was recommended (the two wide and the rotation) did not fit
  together, because the governance set is immutable and with wide leaves «its root changes». The
  author chose between the two branches the session gave him: the two wide and a new constitution.

  ⚠️ **CORRECTION (§683), cited and not erased: the premise of §679 was false.** The map of E2
  measured that a wide key padded with zeros gives EXACTLY the narrow identity
  (`derive_custodian_id_wide([k,0,0,0]) == derive_custodian_id(k)`, and the same in governance and

  the wide governance. There is no deployed ledger (`SECURITY.md` 3.6).

## D-G — A proof says what it is: the v2 mark, with its family, its AIR version and its profile

**Status: proposed (§707); ⚠️ §916: ACCEPTED with the RFC («Aceptar todo» [accept everything],
29-10-2026), built in §914.** The author decided it on 07-10-2026: he accepted in writing, as a
block, the decision proposal that a Claude Code session prepared on `161a233`, with the option it
recommended, the v2 mark with profile at the cut. Five answers to questions that proposal left open
—that the prefix keeps saying `oculta`, that the registry goes to `spec/NUCLEO.md`, that one profile
value stays reserved for another engine, that `digest_pi` also binds the family and the version of
the AIR, and that D-G comes in now, without waiting for the parameters of the cut— the session
proposed after that acceptance, and they hold by it until the author says otherwise. «Proposed» is
the state of D-G as a stage: the decision is taken and written, and nothing of it is built. As in
RFC-0005 and in RFC-0008, the state of each stage lives in its place and that of the document
belongs to the process, so the RFC does not change state. D-G becomes built when its entry seals it,
with its replica and its vectors (rule 4 of the PROCESO), in the seal of the cut and not before.

**What there is today.** A STARK proof of the house carries no identity beyond the hiding
mark: `arqueo:oculta:1` and m, 19 bytes in the meta of its trace (RFC-0009 D-S). The family is
chosen by the caller —the `tipo` of the envelope in the kit, the method in the layer— and the
options are bound by an exact `OptionSet`. When an AIR changes under the same wire version, a proof
of the earlier one fails with a winterfell error, and not with «it is of another version»: within
`zkssl/0.4` it happened three times, and §699 measured it with the send,
`InconsistentOodConstraintEvaluations` between nodes that all say `zkssl/0.4` (`spec/RPC.md`
§«Notas operativas» [operational notes]). The cut changes the bytes of the proof on several sides at
once —E1 and E2 of this RFC, the E3 of RFC-0019 (D-D) and the parameters of D-F—, and without a
named cause the second implementation of milestone H3 only fails like the reference by chance: §706
matched it with the kit on the v1 mark, and v2 has to be born matched. The v2.0 blueprint had
already pointed it out: the Fiat-Shamir seed carries no family identifier, and it proposed one
inside the transcript (`doc/blueprint-v2.md` §«4.3 D3»).

**Decision.** The mark goes to version 2, of 24 fixed bytes: the prefix `arqueo:oculta:2`, m (u32),
the family (u16, from the registry of the spec), the version of the AIR (u16) and the profile (u8,
index of the `OptionSet` table of the spec). The prefix keeps saying `oculta`: every production
proof goes hidden and the kit already reads that prefix. Two things are discarded: committing the
options and the AIR in the head (below, «Why in the mark and not in the head»), and putting the
family only in the public inputs, which would separate the domains but would give no name: a proof
of another version would keep falling without saying why. What does stay open is committing the
profile in the head as well, if one day the operator chooses it per deployment: the profile field
allows it without another mark version.

**The v2 mark, byte by byte.** The mark lives where it lives today, in the meta of the `TraceInfo`,
which the proof serializes at the start: four bytes of widths and length, the length of the meta in
a u16 and the meta (`crates/winter-air/src/air/trace_info.rs`, `write_into`). The head of the proof
ends up so:

| bytes of the proof | field | with v1, today |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | main width, auxiliary width, auxiliary randoms and log2 of the length | the same |
| 4-5 | length of the meta, u16 LE: **24** | 19 |
| 6-20 | the prefix, `arqueo:oculta:2` | `arqueo:oculta:1` |
| 21-24 | m, u32 LE: the degree of the randomizers of the quotient (RFC-0009 D-H) | the same |
| 25-26 | **the family**, u16 LE | — |
| 27-28 | **the version of the AIR**, u16 LE | — |
| 29 | **the profile**, u8 | — |
| 30-38 | the field modulus: its length and its eight bytes | 25-33 |
| 39-48 | the options | 34-43 |

- **The family** is one per AIR with a hidden prover: the 23 of the provers with a row, those that
  call `ocultacion_encendida` (`crates/stark-experiment/src/lib.rs`). All are versioned, also
  those only the node judges, because the node is also a judge: the five the kit judges; the
  fourteen the layer judges —send and claim with their v2, the burn, the refund with its v2, the
  five climbs, the audit and the threshold of the wire—; and the four that today no production
  judge judges (`circuit_threshold`, `double_entry`, `merkle` and `solvency`), whose prover writes
  the mark the same.
- **The version of the AIR** belongs to each family and starts at 1 with the cut. It rises
  according to the extension rule, below.
- **The profile** is the index of the `OptionSet` table of the spec. With a single `OptionSet` per
  wire version it is a constant, redundant with the options of the context; it serves to name the
  rejection and so that a second `OptionSet` —a regulated profile, or one the operator chooses—
  does not ask for another mark version. The value 255 stays reserved for a proof of another
  engine: what it means, and how a proof that does not have winterfell's format is identified, is
  decided by the RFC of layer 2.
- **Zero is not assigned** in any of the three fields, so that a prover that does not declare its
  own does not write a valid mark.
- **The registry** —the families, the accepted AIR versions and the profiles— goes to
  `spec/NUCLEO.md`, which is normative: the identity of a proof is core, not package. It has a
  single producer in the code, like the set of head versions of its section 3, and a mark KAT per
  entry.

**What enters the Fiat-Shamir seed, and why it is enough.** The seed of the public coin is the
context of the proof followed by its public inputs (`crates/winter-verifier/src/lib.rs`,
`verify`: `proof.context.to_elements()` and `pub_inputs.to_elements()`). The context carries first
the `TraceInfo` —one element with the widths, another with the length and the meta in chunks of
seven bytes, one element per chunk (`trace_info.rs`, `to_elements`)— and then the modulus, the
number of constraints and the options (`crates/winter-air/src/proof/context.rs`, `to_elements`).
RFC-0009 D-H measured that touching one byte of the mark leaves the proof unverified.

So, everything v2 puts in the mark enters the seed without touching the channel: changing the
family, the version of the AIR or the profile changes all the challenges. A proof of family X cannot
be relabelled as of family Y, nor one of version 1 as of version 2, without redoing it; and two
families with the same trace shape and the same public inputs stop sharing a seed: it is the domain
separation per family the blueprint proposed (D3). It is enough because the identity only needs
three things, and it has them: that the judge reads it before building the AIR, to name the cause
(below); that it cannot be edited without redoing the proof (the seed binds it); and that it is
read the same everywhere (a fixed format, with a reader in each implementation). The options and the
public inputs do not go in the mark, and stay bound as today: the options, by the context and by
the exact equality with the `OptionSet`; the public inputs, by their own elements of the seed.

The length is fixed per version because the seed carries the meta, in its chunks, but not its
length: the judge rejects any length other than 24, as today `Marca::leer` rejects any other than 19
(`crates/winter-air/src/marca.rs`). In the seed, 24 bytes are ⌈24/7⌉ = 4 elements against today's
3: the context goes from 11 elements to 12, measured with the replica of `tools/segunda/stark.py`
on the proof of `spec/vectors/edad/edad-todos.json` (§707). That the v2 mark ends up bound is
DERIVED from reading the code and from what RFC-0009 D-H measured; E3 measures it with a test of the
fork: the proof with the family of its mark changed, verified with its own AIR without going through
the judge, does not verify.

**What the judge does.** The same thing and in the same order in the kit, in the layer of the node
and in `tools/segunda`, and each step with its named cause, in its class of `spec/PAQUETE.md` §«6.1
La causa del rechazo» [the cause of the rejection]:

1. **The mark, first.** The judge reads the head of the proof —the four bytes, the length of the
   meta and the meta, from byte 6— without deserializing the rest, and judges it before anything:
   before building any AIR and before the statement. In the age that means reading it before
   composing the statement with the head and before climbing the two sub-roots, which today go
   before the proof (`crates/zk-ssl-air/src/lib.rs`, `verificar_contra_cabeza`): if the cut changes
   what the age composes with the head, a `zkssl/0.4` proof would fall by the sub-root and not by
   its mark. The author decided it for the age; the proposal extends it to the kit's five families,
   because in four of them `verificar` today checks the statement before reading the proof, and in
   the claim and the payment `verificar_contra_cabeza` first compares `nacido` with the head.
2. **The statement**, as today: `comprobar_enunciado`; in the age, composed with the head and with
   the climbed sub-roots; in the claim and the payment, with `nacido` earlier than the head.
3. **The proof**: it is deserialized whole; its options have to be those of its profile; its trace
   shape, that of its family; and only then `verify`.

Today the judges of the kit go statement —except the pledge—, proof, shape, mark and `verify`, which
validates the options before anything; the second one, the same since §706. With D-G they go mark,
statement, options and shape. What is rejected at each step:

| step | what it rejects | proposed text | class (§6.1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1a | there is no head: fewer than six bytes, or a meta that does not fit | `la prueba no se deserializa` | malformed proof |
| 1b | the empty meta, a proof earlier than `zkssl/0.4` | `marca: meta vacio, prueba anterior a zkssl/0.4; la juzga el kit de su version` | outside the contract |

The texts and the classes are a proposal: E3 fixes them in the catalogue of section 5 of
`spec/PAQUETE.md` and in its 6.1, with its vectors. They go in ASCII, like today's, and all those of
the mark start with `marca`, so that a manifest pins the cause and not only the subject. 1a and 1h
stay in today's class of the mark, «malformed proof» (§706); those that say another version —or
a mark that is not known, 1d—, another family or another AIR go to «outside the contract», the one
of «a version or a type the command does not read». A v1 mark of another length, which today falls
as «malformed proof» with `Err(Largo)`, thus moves to 1d. In the node, the layer answers
`VerificationFailed` with the same text, which comes out of a single producer in `zk-ssl-air`, like
`comprobar_marca` today.

**The second implementation.** `tools/segunda/stark.py` replicates it with the same order, the same
checks and the same text: `lectura_de_marca` reads v2 and names v1 and the empty meta like kit
0.5; `comprobar_marca` receives the AIR, and each class of `tools/segunda/airs.py` declares as

catalogue of `spec/PAQUETE.md`), which is what the E5 criterion of RFC-0005 asks: writing it from
the spec and not from the code.

**Extension rule.** The version of the AIR rises if its constraints, its width, the length of its
trace —or the rule that fixes it—, its auxiliary segment or its public inputs change; the profile,
if the options change; the version of the mark, if anything else the judge has to know changes: the
construction of the hiding, the hash, the vector commitment, the coin or the format of the bytes.
Any of the three is a new wire version, with new vectors and the earlier ones preserved under
theirs (rule 2 of the PROCESO). The rule versions proofs: what is not a proof —the leaf of the
reception receipt, the head— carries its own version.

**Why in the mark and not in the head.** Committing the options and the AIR in the head —a new
`paramsDigest`, or a field of a new head— would leave the proof without an identity of its own:
those of send, claim, burn and threshold are judged by the node without a head. It would not enter
Fiat-Shamir. And it would force the judge to accept what the head declares, with a floor: it is the
downgrade D-E closes, and a minimum-level policy that no verifier of the house uses (§697). If one
day the operator chooses the profile per deployment, the head will be able to commit it as well,
without another mark version.

**Compatibility.** Kit 0.5 rejects by name the proofs without a mark (those of `zkssl/0.3`) and
those with the v1 mark (those of `zkssl/0.4`), and does not verify them, by D-E. The evidence of
those versions is judged by their kit, which is still published and is rebuilt from its commit (⚠️ §823: it said «and it is reproducible (`tools/artefacto.sh --check`)»: the `--check` compiles the kit of the tree, not that of a published commit, and with the same fingerprint it is only shown within one same machine), and its vectors
move to their version with their bytes and their fingerprints (rule 2 of the PROCESO). Kit 0.4.x
does not verify a 0.5 proof: it only accepts the v1 mark with m = 64. But it does not always reject
it by the mark, because it looks first at the shape of the trace, like the second one since §706:
depending on what the cut changes, it falls by its shape, by its mark or, in the age, by the
statement it composes with the head.

**Cost.** Five bytes per proof and one more element in the seed.

**Why now.** Without versioning the proofs, the proof system cannot change without breaking third
parties without telling them why, and within `zkssl/0.4` it already happened three times (§699).
The cut changes the bytes of all proofs on several sides at once, and it is the break that is paid
once. A layer 2 other than the winterfell fork —the upstream has no commit on `main` since
19-07-2025, measured on 01-10-2026 (`doc/integracion-vertical-evaluacion.md` §«4. Integración
vertical» [vertical integration])— will also need to name its engine. And the second implementation
of milestone H3 only fails like the reference if the cause has a name and the two look for it in the
same order.

## Security

The spending key does not travel and this RFC does not touch it. E1 gives back to the AIR a
guarantee that today only the layer gives; E2 raises the cost of forcing a custodian key from about
2^63 to about 2^255 evaluations (ESTIMATED, by the size of the `Digest`). D-G, if it is built,
touches neither the spending key nor what a proof proves: it puts its identity in the seed, which
separates the domains by family and by AIR version, and makes what today falls without a name in
verification be rejected earlier, with a name.
